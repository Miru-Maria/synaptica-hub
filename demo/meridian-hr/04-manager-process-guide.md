# Manager Process Guide — Performance & Development

**Audience:** People Managers  
**Owner:** People Operations  
**Version:** 2.1 — Updated August 2023

---

## Overview

This guide covers the end-to-end process for managing your team's performance on Meridian HR. It supplements the in-platform help articles and is intended to give managers the "why" behind each step, not just the "how."

---

## The quarterly review rhythm

Meridian HR structures team performance around a quarterly cadence:

- **Q1 (January–March):** Goal-setting and alignment
- **Q2 (April–June):** Mid-year check-in
- **Q3 (July–September):** Goal progress review
- **Q4 (October–December):** Annual performance assessment

Each quarter ends with a structured review session. For Q2 and Q4, these are formal evaluation periods with written assessments. Q1 and Q3 are lighter check-ins with no formal rating.

> **Note:** The employee onboarding guide states that reviews happen bi-annually (June and December only). This guide reflects the current quarterly process. Please use this document as the authoritative source.

---

## Before the evaluation period opens

Two weeks before each formal evaluation period, People Operations will send a calendar invite with:
- The opening and closing dates for self-assessments
- The calibration session date for your team
- Any changes to the competency framework for this cycle

**Your pre-cycle checklist:**
- [ ] Review and update each team member's approved goals (Goals should have been set at the start of the quarter)
- [ ] Identify any team members who may need a PIP conversation this cycle
- [ ] Confirm all direct reports are added to your team in Meridian HR under **Team Settings**

---

## Running the self-assessment phase

Once the evaluation period opens, employees will receive an email asking them to complete their self-assessment within 10 days.

During this phase, you should:
- **Not** discuss ratings with your direct reports (to avoid anchoring their self-assessment)
- Be available to answer clarifying questions about goals or competencies
- Monitor completion via the **Progress** tab in the cycle overview

---

## Completing your manager assessment

After the self-assessment deadline closes, you will have access to each employee's self-assessment. You then complete your own parallel assessment.

**Best practices:**
- Review the employee's goal progress before assigning ratings
- Use concrete examples from the quarter to justify each rating
- For 360 assessments, peer ratings are visible in aggregate but not attributed to individuals
- Pay attention to significant gaps between self-assessment and your rating — these should be addressed in the feedback conversation

Rating scale:
- **1 — Below expectations:** Performance is not meeting the minimum requirements for the role
- **2 — Developing:** Partially meeting expectations; clear growth areas identified
- **3 — Meeting expectations:** Consistently performing at the level required
- **4 — Exceeding expectations:** Regularly goes beyond what is required
- **5 — Outstanding:** Exceptional, sustained performance that sets a benchmark for the team

---

## The calibration session

After all manager assessments are submitted, your leadership team will hold a calibration session. The purpose is to ensure rating consistency across managers and teams.

During calibration:
- You will present a brief summary for each of your direct reports
- The group may challenge or adjust ratings where there is clear inconsistency
- Any adjustments are made by you in the platform after the session — changes are logged in the audit trail
- Calibration Mode (if enabled in your account settings) will weight your manager rating more heavily in 360 reviews

---

## Releasing results to employees

After calibration, you release results to each employee individually. Do not release all results at once — schedule 1:1 feedback conversations first.

To release results:
1. Navigate to the cycle overview.
2. Click **Release Results** next to the employee's name.
3. The employee will receive an in-app notification and email.

**Important:** Once released, results cannot be un-released. Ensure ratings are final before releasing.

---

## Performance Improvement Plans (PIPs)

If an employee receives a rating of 1 ("Below expectations"), you should initiate a PIP conversation with the support of People Operations. PIPs are managed outside Meridian HR using the PIP template provided by People Ops.

Contact People Operations before beginning any PIP process. Do not document PIP details inside the platform.

---

## Development plans

After results are released, create a Development Plan for each team member in the platform:

1. Navigate to the employee's profile.
2. Click **Development → Create Plan**.
3. Add 2–3 focus areas based on the review outcomes.
4. Set a review date for the plan (recommended: end of next quarter).

Development plans are visible to the employee and their direct manager only.
