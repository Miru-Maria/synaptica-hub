# New Employee Onboarding Guide — Meridian HR Platform

**Owner:** People Operations  
**Applies to:** All new hires  
**Last reviewed:** March 2022

---

## Welcome

This guide will help you get set up on Meridian HR during your first week. You'll use Meridian HR to track your goals, participate in assessment cycles, give and receive feedback, and manage your development plans.

---

## Week 1 checklist

- [ ] Accept the platform invite sent to your work email
- [ ] Complete your profile (name, role, department, start date, profile photo)
- [ ] Set your first 3 goals (see "Setting Goals" below)
- [ ] Connect with your line manager in-platform by navigating to **People → My Manager**
- [ ] Review any open feedback requests in your **Inbox**
- [ ] Confirm your department in **Profile Settings** — this affects which review templates are assigned to you

---

## Setting goals

Goals in Meridian HR are tied to your quarterly OKRs and reviewed during each evaluation period.

To set a goal:
1. Go to **Goals** in the left sidebar.
2. Click **New Goal**.
3. Enter a title, a description, and a target completion date.
4. Assign a **Key Result** (optional but recommended).
5. Tag your goal with a **Focus Area**: Leadership, Delivery, Growth, or Collaboration.
6. Click **Save**.

Your manager will be notified and can approve, comment on, or edit your goals. Goals cannot be submitted for review until they are approved by your Team Lead.

> **Tip:** Keep goal titles short and outcome-focused. Instead of "Work on communication," write "Deliver weekly written project updates to stakeholders by Friday."

---

## Understanding your review cycle

Every employee at Meridian HR participates in a bi-annual assessment cycle — one in June and one in December. Your manager will initiate the cycle from their side; you will receive an email when it opens.

During an assessment cycle you will be asked to:
- Complete a **self-assessment** (rated 1–5 on each competency)
- Reflect on your goals from the past period
- Optionally nominate peers for 360 feedback

Results are released to you after the calibration session. You will receive an in-app notification when your results are available.

---

## Giving feedback

You can give unsolicited feedback to any colleague at any time:

1. Go to **Feedback → Give Feedback**.
2. Search for the colleague by name.
3. Choose a **feedback type**: Recognition, Developmental, or Project-based.
4. Write your feedback (minimum 50 characters).
5. Choose visibility: **Visible to recipient and manager** or **Manager only**.
6. Submit.

---

## Your development plan

After your first assessment cycle, your manager will create a Development Plan for you in the platform. This outlines agreed-upon growth areas and recommended actions for the next period. You can view your plan under **Development → My Plan**.
