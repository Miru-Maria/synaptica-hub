## The Problem: Knowledge That Doesn't Work

Every growing organization hits a point where knowledge management breaks down. The symptoms are familiar:

- **Fragmented documentation.** Information is spread across multiple platforms with no clear structure. Teams duplicate effort because they can't find what already exists.
- **Tribal knowledge.** Critical processes live in people's heads. When those people leave, take vacation, or simply get busy, the knowledge goes with them.
- **Search that fails.** Internal search tools return too many irrelevant results or miss documents entirely. People stop trusting the system and fall back on asking colleagues directly.
- **AI readiness gaps.** Organizations that want to adopt AI assistants or chatbots discover that their existing documentation is too messy, incomplete, or poorly structured for AI to use effectively.

These aren't minor inconveniences. They slow down onboarding, increase support costs, create compliance risks, and limit the value you can extract from AI investments. The root cause is almost always the same: there's no deliberate architecture behind how knowledge is organized and accessed.

## Synaptica's Approach

Synaptica takes a systems-level view of knowledge. Rather than writing more documents or adding another tool to your stack, we work on the underlying structure — the taxonomy, retrieval logic, and content strategy that determine whether your knowledge systems actually help people find answers.

Our approach is built on three principles:

**1. Start with what exists.** We don't ask you to throw everything out and start over. We audit your current documentation, identify what's working and what's missing, and build from there.

**2. Design for both humans and machines.** Knowledge systems that work for human readers but can't be parsed by AI are incomplete. Systems optimized purely for AI retrieval but unreadable by people are useless. We design for both.

**3. Make it sustainable.** A knowledge architecture that requires constant manual curation to stay useful will eventually be abandoned. We design systems with clear ownership, governance, and tooling so they stay current without heroic effort.

A retainer engagement provides continuous access to Synaptica for knowledge systems design, documentation strategy, and AI implementation. This is the right fit if you're building or rebuilding core knowledge infrastructure and want a dedicated partner through the process.

### KA Sprint

**A focused engagement to solve a specific knowledge architecture problem.**

The KA Sprint is a structured, time-boxed engagement — typically two to four weeks — designed to produce a concrete deliverable. It's the right choice when you have a well-defined challenge and need expert input to move forward.

Common Sprint outcomes include:

- A complete taxonomy and topic architecture for a knowledge base or documentation site
- A gap analysis identifying what's missing, what's redundant, and what needs restructuring
- A retrieval strategy for AI-assisted search — making sure AI tools can find and use the right content from your documents
- A structured set of prompt templates for a specific knowledge workflow

Each Sprint follows a clear process: scoping, analysis, design, and handover. You receive a documented deliverable — not just recommendations, but an artifact your team can implement immediately.

### Prompt Engineering Workshop

**Practical prompt design for teams building AI-powered knowledge tools.**

This workshop is for teams that are already using or planning to use large language models in their workflows and need a structured, repeatable approach to prompt design.

The workshop covers:

- Prompt library creation — designing, testing, and documenting prompt templates for your specific use cases
- Reusable prompt templates with fill-in-the-blank fields so prompts can be adapted across contexts
- Style guide development — consistent tone, structure, and formatting rules that get appended to every prompt
- Live testing against real models with your content
- Handover documentation so your team can maintain and extend the library independently

This is hands-on, not theoretical. We build the actual prompts your team will use, test them with your content, and leave you with a working system.

## Client-Facing Tools

Synaptica has built a suite of working AI tools that demonstrate our approach and provide immediate value. Two tools in particular are central to how we work with clients.

### DocAudit

**Automated documentation gap analysis.**

DocAudit is a tool that analyzes your existing documentation and tells you exactly where the gaps are. You provide your content — via file upload, pasted text, URL, or Notion integration — and DocAudit does the rest.

Here's how it works:

1. **Ingest.** Submit your documentation in whatever format you have it. DocAudit accepts PDF, DOCX, Markdown, and plain text files. You can also point it at a URL or connect a Notion workspace.
2. **Configure.** Select a topic taxonomy — we provide four presets covering common knowledge domains, or you can define custom topics relevant to your organization.
3. **Analyze.** DocAudit breaks your content into sections, compares coverage against your selected topics using AI, and identifies which areas are well-covered, which have partial coverage, and which are missing entirely.
4. **Report.** You receive a detailed gap report with coverage scores, a radar chart visualization, severity-ranked findings, and actionable recommendations. The report can be exported as a branded PDF.

DocAudit is available for free on the Synaptica website — no login required. It's also the starting point for many client engagements, because it gives both sides a concrete, data-driven picture of where the documentation stands.

### Learning OS

**A structured learning platform for AI knowledge architecture.**

The Learning OS is a self-paced learning platform for professionals who want to build skills in AI knowledge architecture. It's designed for knowledge managers, information architects, team leads, and AI-curious professionals who want to lead the AI transition in their organizations — not just observe it.

The platform includes five integrated modules:

- **Skill Map** — Visualize your competencies across AI, knowledge architecture, and systems thinking. Track growth and identify gaps.
- **Learning Paths** — Three progressive tiers of structured curriculum: AI Transition fundamentals, Knowledge Systems design, and Expert Practice.
- **Project Tracker** — Apply what you learn through guided projects. Set milestones and build a portfolio of real work.
- **Knowledge Notes** — Capture insights and build a personal knowledge base as you learn.
- **AI Tutor** — An AI assistant trained on the curriculum that answers questions, explains concepts, and guides your learning.

The Learning OS is available in tiered plans, from a free Explorer tier to a full-access Expert tier. It reflects Synaptica's broader belief that the organizations that will get the most value from AI are the ones that invest in building internal expertise — not just buying tools.
