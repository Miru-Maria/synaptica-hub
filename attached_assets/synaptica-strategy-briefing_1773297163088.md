## 2. Marketing Strategy & Positioning

### The Core Positioning

Synaptica Knowledge Systems sits at the intersection of three capabilities that are almost never held by the same person:

- **Technical depth** — vector search, RAG pipelines, prompt engineering, database architecture
- **Strategic thinking** — information architecture, taxonomy design, knowledge structure
- **Communication craft** — technical writing, documentation strategy, content design

Most AI engineers cannot do the strategy and writing. Most documentation specialists cannot build the technical systems. This combination is the unique value proposition and should be the central message across all marketing touchpoints.

### The Three-Layer Business Structure

**Layer 1 — The Lab (Free Tools)**
Interactive tools hosted publicly. The entry point for every potential client. Try before you buy — and proof that the methodology works before a single meeting is booked.

**Layer 2 — Services (Bespoke Engagements)**
Consulting engagements: short audits, medium-length builds, ongoing retainers. Priced for small-to-mid organizations and solo-operator delivery speed.

**Layer 3 — Future Products**
If demand proves out: white-labeled RAG chatbot, monthly knowledge health report, or a prompt library subscription. Tools evolve into micro-SaaS.

### Messaging Hierarchy

- **Headline:** "I design the intelligence infrastructure of modern organizations"
- **Sub-message:** Knowledge architecture, RAG pipelines, and documentation strategy for teams building with AI
- **Proof:** The Lab — five live, interactive tools you can use right now, no login required
- **Call to action:** Work With Me — select freelance engagements, Q2 2026 availability

## 4. Target Buyers

Prioritize buyers where knowledge chaos is a recognized, funded problem — not a nice-to-have. The following segments have the highest conversion likelihood for a solo knowledge architecture practice.

### SaaS Companies
Product documentation, support knowledge base, internal onboarding. Knowledge is a customer-retention and support-cost lever — the business case is easy to articulate.

### Consultancies & Agencies
Internal knowledge capture, proposal intelligence, methodology documentation. High value placed on structured knowledge; often underserved internally.

### Startups (20–100 people)
Pre-scale knowledge architecture before tribal knowledge becomes a liability. High urgency and willingness to pay for fast, expert-led help.

### HR & People Ops Teams
Onboarding systems, role transition, knowledge transfer. The Onboarding Assistant is a direct proof-of-concept for this buyer segment.

### Remote-First Companies
Documentation is the only source of truth across time zones. Every investment in knowledge systems is a productivity multiplier.

### Geographic Targeting

As a Romanian PFA operating remotely, you are not limited to Romanian clients. Your site, tools, and communications are in English and positioned for an international market. UK, US, and Western European clients are all accessible and typically pay significantly higher rates than local market equivalents. **Position at international rates from day one.**

## 5. Trademark Registration Guide

A PFA (Persoană Fizică Autorizată) in Romania is fully eligible to register trademarks. You do not need to operate as an SRL or other company structure. The trademark protects the brand name and exists separately from your official PFA registered name with ANAF.

### Step 1 — Run a Clearance Search First

Before filing, verify that "Synaptica" and "Synaptica Knowledge Systems" are not already registered or pending in the relevant Nice classes. This is free and takes approximately 10 minutes.

- **EUIPO TMview** (covers EU and many other offices): [tmview.europa.eu](https://www.tmview.europa.eu)
- **OSIM national search**: [osim.ro](https://www.osim.ro)

Search for "Synaptica" as a word mark across all classes, then narrow to your relevant ones. If it comes back clear, proceed to filing.

### Step 2 — Choose Your Route

| Route | Coverage | Approx. Cost | Timeline |
|-------|----------|-------------|----------|
| **OSIM (Romania)** | Romania only | RON 420–500 / class | 6–12 months |
| **EUIPO (European Union)** | All 27 EU member states | €850 / class | 4–6 months |

**Recommendation:** For a practice positioned at international and EU clients, the EUIPO route provides substantially stronger protection and higher credibility. The cost difference is minor relative to a single client engagement and the mark is immediately defensible across all EU markets.

### Step 3 — Identify the Correct Nice Classes

You will likely need to file in one or more of the following:

- **Class 42** — Software as a service, AI services, technology consultancy, computer system design
- **Class 41** — Training, workshops, and educational services in AI and knowledge management
- **Class 35** — Business consulting, professional advisory services, knowledge management consulting

A trademark attorney or agent can advise which specific class descriptions best cover your actual services. The wording matters for enforcement scope.

### Step 4 — File and Monitor

Filing can be done directly online through OSIM or EUIPO. However, a Romanian IP attorney or trademark agent can often file on your behalf at a modest flat fee (commonly €200–400 on top of official fees), and their class descriptions tend to be more defensible. They will also monitor for conflicting applications after registration.

### Key Points to Remember

- Your PFA fiscal code (CIF) is used as the applicant identifier — not your personal CNP
- The trademark protects the brand name, independent of your PFA registered name with ANAF
- Once registered, you hold exclusive commercial rights in the registered classes and geography, including the right to challenge infringing uses
- Registration also makes it significantly easier to defend the corresponding domain name and social handles if challenged
- **This document is general orientation and not legal advice.** Verify current fees and procedures with OSIM, EUIPO, or a qualified IP attorney before filing
